The person
behind the path.

I’m a medical student interested in what happens when healthcare reaches beyond the hospital.

Currently in my sixth year at the University of Ilorin, I’m building a foundation in medicine while developing a deeper interest in public health. I expect to graduate in 2027.

Alongside my studies, I’ve gained experience coordinating community outreaches and promoting health education. Sexual and reproductive health is a particular focus: helping people access the knowledge they need to make informed decisions.

I hope to bring clinical knowledge, evidence-based advocacy, and community understanding into the same practice.
