# Visual system
The portfolio uses a botanical green, ivory, and pale citron palette, with Manrope for clear modern typography and Georgia italic for human emphasis. Generous whitespace and an arched photographic portrait make the opening recognisable. Content moves through biography, a photographic field journal, interests, education, personal video, and contact.

Tokens: ink #173c31, paper #f7f8f2, muted #5e6c63, lime #d8e9a1, sage #edf0e5. Buttons have restrained 5px corners; photograph containers have 4px corners. The single arched hero portrait is the exception. Desktop maximum content width is 1280px, mobile content width 88%. Mobile navigation remains visible. Reduced motion disables the portrait reveal and slow circular text animation.

All photographic and video assets originate in the user-supplied folder. WebP derivatives preserve the originals outside the project. No stock or generated imagery, fabricated metrics, or testimonials are used.
